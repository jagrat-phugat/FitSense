# 🏋️‍♀️ FitSense

A full-featured **Fitness Tracker App** that helps users stay on top of their health goals. With integrated BMI calculation, contact support, and plans for future upgrades like online courses and calorie tracking, this app is your digital fitness companion!

---

###  Core Functionalities

- **BMI Calculator**
  - Input height and weight to get your Body Mass Index.
  - Provides health tips based on your BMI category (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese).

- **Contact Support**
  - Built-in contact form to connect with support or trainers.
  - Email/message functionality for queries, feedback, or personalized help.

- **Exercise & Workout Library**
  - A categorized list of exercises: strength, cardio, yoga, etc.
  - Track your daily/weekly workouts.

- **Calorie & Nutrition Tracking**
  - Log meals, count calories, and monitor macronutrients.
  - Recommendations for diet plans and hydration.

---

##  Future Improvements

We're actively working on expanding the app with the following powerful features:

- **Floating Fitness Courses**
  - Courses by certified online trainers.
  - Self-paced workout plans with video and live sessions.

- **Community & Social Features**
  - Friend system, leaderboards, and workout challenges.

## 📦 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React / HTML / CSS / Tailwind (optional)
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: MongoDB (Mongoose)
- **Other**: EmailJS / Nodemailer for contact forms, JWT for user auth (optional)
